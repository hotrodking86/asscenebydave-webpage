About Dave

Hi, I’m Dave, and welcome to As Scene by Dave.

The name is a play on the phrase “As Seen by Dave.” I changed “seen” to “scene” because so much of what I enjoy revolves around Hollywood, movies, filming locations, famous landmarks, and places connected to unforgettable stories.

I’ve been visiting cemeteries for years and have always been fascinated by them. I enjoy learning about the people buried there and discovering the stories behind the names carved into the headstones.

Over time, that interest grew into exploring historic locations, forgotten landmarks, roadside attractions, filming locations, and places most people may pass without ever knowing the stories behind them.

My goal is not simply to show where these places are. I want to share what happened there, who was connected to them, and why they still matter.

Whether it’s the final resting place of a Hollywood legend, a location seen in a favorite movie, or a piece of history slowly disappearing, I enjoy seeing it for myself and sharing it with you.

Thank you for being here and following along. I’m truly glad you’re here.

Dave
