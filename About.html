About As Scene by Dave

History, Hollywood, true crime, cemeteries, filming locations, and the stories hidden in the places all around us.

Welcome

Welcome! I’m Dave, and I’m really glad you’re here.

I’ve lived in Southern California my entire life, and I’ve always been fascinated by the stories hidden in the places all around us. Long before I ever picked up a camera, I spent years visiting cemeteries simply because I wanted to learn more about the people buried there and the history they left behind. That curiosity eventually grew into something much bigger.

Today, As Scene by Dave is my way of sharing those discoveries with you.

When I travel, especially around Southern California and beyond, I like to film the interesting and often overlooked places I come across. Whether it’s a famous Hollywood filming location, the home of a legendary actor, a historic cemetery, a true crime site, or even a roadside stop most people pass without noticing, I try to capture what makes it worth remembering.

I don’t believe every story needs to be sensationalized. Sometimes the most meaningful places are the quiet ones. The ones where the history is still there if you take a moment to look.

What you’ll find here is simple, honest storytelling, thoughtfully researched history, and a look at what these locations actually look like today. Some are beautifully preserved, some have changed completely, and others are slowly fading away.

My goal isn’t just to tell you what happened. It’s to take you there.

Whether you’re into Hollywood history, true crime, historic cemeteries, abandoned places, roadside attractions, or just the stories behind everyday locations, I hope you’ll find something here that sparks your curiosity.

Thanks for stopping by and joining me on this journey.

I’m always on the lookout for the next story worth telling, and I’m glad you’re here to come along for the ride.

Why “As Scene by Dave”?

People sometimes ask about the name.

Originally, it was going to be As Seen by Dave because every video is my perspective, showing these places as I experience them.

But the more I thought about it, the more I realized that many of the stories I enjoy are connected to Hollywood, movies, television, and famous filming locations. Changing “Seen” to “Scene” felt like the perfect fit.

It’s a simple play on words that reflects both what I do and what I love.

Every location has a story to tell, and I hope you’ll enjoy seeing them... As Scene by Dave.

One More Thing...

You may notice that I occasionally stumble over my words. I’ve had a stutter for as long as I can remember, and it still pops up from time to time.

I could edit every pause and restart out of my videos, but I’d rather be myself. What you see is me, and I hope that’s part of what makes these stories feel real.
